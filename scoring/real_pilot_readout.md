# 2026-10-26 real-transcript provenance pilot

Source: three hand-selected, English, non-toxic, non-redacted multi-turn conversations from [WildChat-1M](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/WildChat-1M), dataset revision `7d6490e462285cf85d91eabea0f9a954fbddcd1f`. They are real user–assistant interactions, unlike the generated BEAM conversations. `real_source_manifest.json` pins row numbers 40060, 425041, and 75054 plus the conversation and content hashes. The imported data has 12 ordered turn sessions and 24 unique, role-qualified message IDs; all three reconstructed conversation digests match the source manifest. Raw message text, model outputs, and quote-level CSVs remain local and Git-ignored.

Run: `runs/real-wildchat/20261026-003630`; local audit artifacts: `scoring/real_write_sheet.csv`, `scoring/real_write_sheet_key.csv`, `scoring/real_trace_sheet.csv`, and `scoring/real_trace_scores.csv`.

Nine writers processed all 12 sessions: 108/108 valid JSON checkpoints, with no transport or schema failures. Applied add/update operations yielded 36 candidate writes. The independent Gemini trace judge scored 36/36, with no errors: 17 fully supported by user messages, 17 partially supported, and 2 unsupported. Its 86 cited evidence snippets were independently checked as exact substrings of the referenced **user** messages. Both unsupported cases in the local writer were spot-checked and describe implementation details supplied by the earlier assistant rather than by the user. Several partial cases likewise mixed a user request with assistant-proposed implementation specifics.

This checks write provenance, not whether the writer remembered everything important. Gemini 4 Argon and Grok 4.7 produced no applied writes in these conversations, so they do not appear in the write-only trace table; zero writes is not evidence of good recall. The source rows are a convenience sample, not a random or representative estimate. The support labels are model judgments, and only the two unsupported cases received a targeted human spot-check; human review of all partial/support labels and a recall assessment are still needed before public comparative claims.

At the configured list prices, estimated hosted writer cost was $0.475372 for 140,647 input and 15,021 output tokens. This excludes local compute and trace-judge calls. See `real_trace_report.md` for per-model counts. The source manifest and this readout deliberately omit conversation text and IP-derived metadata.
